Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how MoneyMinded ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit moneymindedguide.com (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. The information we may collect falls into three categories:

  • Information you give us. If you contact us through our contact form or by email, we collect the name, email address, and any details you choose to include in your message.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our servers and service providers automatically log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring pages, and the dates and times of your visits.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies to make the Site work, understand how it is used, and (where applicable) show relevant advertising. See the sections below.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to operate and maintain the Site, respond to your messages, understand how visitors use our content so we can improve it, keep the Site secure and prevent abuse, and display advertising that helps keep our content free.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them to remember your preferences and to gather analytics about how the Site is used. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and you can set most browsers to refuse cookies. If you disable cookies, some parts of the Site may not function as intended.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We display advertising to keep MoneyMinded free to read. Our advertising is served in part by third-party vendors, including Google.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other sites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our Site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu (for EU users).

For more information about how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, please see Google's Privacy & Terms. If you would like more information about Google AdSense, you can review Google's advertising policies at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to help us understand aggregate visitor behaviour, such as which pages are most popular. These services may set their own cookies and process usage data on our behalf. The information is used in aggregate and is not used to personally identify you.

Third-party links

Our articles sometimes link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any website you visit.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, and the right to opt out of certain uses. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a US state with applicable privacy laws (such as California), you may exercise these rights by contacting us using the details below. You can also manage advertising preferences using the opt-out links above.

Children's privacy

MoneyMinded is intended for a general, adult audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at hello@moneymindedguide.com or through our contact page.
