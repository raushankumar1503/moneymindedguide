How our calculators work

Each tool uses a standard, published formula and rounds results for readability. Nothing you type is sent anywhere — the maths happens on your device, and we do not store your numbers. Results are estimates for illustration: real outcomes differ because of changing prices, interest, taxes, fees, and your own circumstances. For a decision that matters, treat the output as a starting point and, where appropriate, speak to a qualified professional. You can read how we research and check our work in our editorial standards.
