Founder & Editor, MoneyMinded

Raushan Kumar is the founder and editor of MoneyMinded, a personal-finance education website focused on making everyday money topics easier to understand. He oversees the site's educational content, research process, source verification, and editorial standards.

Raushan is not a licensed financial adviser, accountant, or tax professional, and MoneyMinded does not present him as one. His aim is to translate mainstream, widely accepted financial principles into clear, practical steps — never to give personalized advice.

Editorial & research responsibilities

As founder and editor, Raushan is responsible for what MoneyMinded publishes. That includes choosing which topics to cover, researching each guide from reputable and official sources, verifying important figures and claims, editing drafts for accuracy and clarity, and updating guides when guidance changes. MoneyMinded may use AI-assisted tools during drafting or editing, but published content is reviewed and edited under the site's process, and a person is always accountable for it. You can read the full detail in our editorial standards.
